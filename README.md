# Spool & Lure

Free, metric-first calculators for spinning and lure anglers. Everything is in millimetres, metres and grams. No sign-up, no cookies, no analytics.

Live site: https://danilojmaric.github.io/spool-and-lure/

## Tools

- **Line capacity & backing** - how much line fits a spool, and how much backing to put under the main line.
- **PE line size converter** - PE and gou numbers to diameter (mm) and breaking strength (kg).
- **Lure weight & rod match** - check which lures suit a rod's casting weight.

## Good to know

- Results are estimates. Your tackle's own ratings always win.
- PE diameters follow the JAFS standard (PE 1.0 = 0.165 mm). Breaking strengths are typical ranges, not guarantees.
- The "sweet spot" in the lure tool is a rule of thumb (middle half of the rod's casting range), not an industry standard.
