Privacy

Short version: this site has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and the numbers you type into the calculators never leave your device.

What happens in your browser

Every calculator runs on your device. The line diameters, lengths and weights you enter are not sent to me or to anyone else.

The site stores two small items in your browser, and nothing else:

  • your light or dark theme choice, so the page looks the same next time;
  • for a moment, the line diameter you carry from the PE converter into the capacity calculator.

Neither item leaves your device. You can clear them any time in your browser settings.

What the site does not use

No cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no sign-up and no accounts. The fonts are served from this site, not from Google or another font service.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address and browser type when a page is loaded. I don't receive or see that data. How GitHub handles it is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Tips on Ko-fi

If you leave a tip, you do it on Ko-fi, which is a separate service with its own privacy policy. Payments are handled by Ko-fi and PayPal, so I never see your card or PayPal details. I can see what Ko-fi shows creators, such as your display name, the amount, and your message if you write one.

Email

If you email me, for example to report a wrong number, I use your address only to reply, and keep the message only as long as needed to deal with it. My address: danilo.maric.office [at] gmail.com

Your rights

Because the site doesn't collect personal data itself, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed me or left a tip and want something removed, write to me and I'll handle it. If you're in the EU or EEA, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Who runs this site

Spool & Lure is a personal, non-commercial project run by Danilo in Serbia. If that ever changes, for example if I add analytics or ads, I will update this page first.
