How PE and gou sizes work

Japanese line makers size line by a number called gou. Braid uses the same scale with a PE prefix. The number fixes the diameter: PE 1.0 and No. 1 fluoro are both about 0.165 mm, and diameter grows with the square root of the number.

diameter ≈ 0.165 mm × √PE

Strength is a different story. Braid strength at the same PE size depends on strand count and brand: 8-strand braid packs more fibre into the same diameter, so it rates higher than budget 4-strand. Mono follows a simpler rule of about 4 lb (1.8 kg) per number up to No. 5, a little less above that. Fluoro runs slightly weaker than mono. Always trust the rating on your own spool over any chart.

Sources: VARIVAS, Japanese line size standard (diameters) · Fishing Line Guide, PE rating chart (braid strength) · Japan Fishing and Tackle News (mono strength).

FAQ

What does PE mean on braided line?

PE stands for polyethylene, the fibre braid is made from. The number after it is a size on the Japanese standard, not a breaking strength. PE 1.0 is about 0.165 mm.

Why do two PE 1.0 braids have different strengths?

The PE number fixes thickness, not strength. Fibre grade, 4 or 8 strands, and how each brand tests its line all change the rated strength.

Is a gou number the same as a PE number?

They share the diameter scale, so No. 1 fluoro and PE 1.0 braid are both about 0.165 mm. Mono is much weaker at the same size, roughly 4 lb (1.8 kg) per number up to No. 5.
